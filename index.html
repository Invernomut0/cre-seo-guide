--- ## Phase 1: Property Screening & Due Diligence The first phase is finding and qualifying deals. This is where most CRE agents waste time. ### Step 1.1: Pull property data and ownership history Use AI to query property records, ownership chains, and zoning information. Instead of manually cross-referencing county assessor sites, MLS listings, and tax records, AI tools can aggregate this into a structured summary in minutes. **What to ask your AI:** > "Pull comparable commercial property sales in [area] for [property type] in the last 12 months. Summarize price-per-sqft ranges, days-on-market, and any unusual ownership patterns." ### Step 1.2: Zone and use-case compatibility check Not all properties can be used for all purposes. Zoning restrictions, environmental constraints, and physical characteristics (ceiling height, loading docks, parking ratio) filter deals fast. **Workflow tip:** Feed the property address and your target use case into an AI tool. Ask it to flag zoning mismatches, environmental red flags, and physical property characteristics that would disqualify the deal. --- ## Phase 2: Investment Analysis (Cap Rate, NOI, Cash-on-Cash) This is the technical core — and where enterprise software ($50–200/month) and generic spreadsheets both fall short. ### Step 2.1: NOI calculation from rent rolls and operating statements Commercial properties generate income through complex rent structures: base rent, CAM reimbursements, percentage rent, escalations. Manual extraction is error-prone and slow. **AI workflow:** 1. Upload the rent roll (PDF, Excel, or even photos of documents) 2. Ask the AI to extract: base rents, CAM charges, tenant escalations, lease expirations 3. Request a structured NOI projection (stabilized, year 1, year 3) ### Step 2.2: Cap rate and return metrics The AI should then compute: - **Cap Rate** = NOI / Purchase Price - **Cash-on-Cash Return** (if leveraged) - **IRR projection** over your hold period - **Sensitivity analysis** (what happens if vacancy rises 3%?) ### Step 2.3: Comparable deal benchmarking Here's what most agents miss: comparing your target Cap Rate against recently closed deals, not just active listings. Active listings are optimistic; closed deals are what the market actually pays. **Ask your AI:** > "Given this property's NOI of $[X] and asking price of $[Y], what is the cap rate? Compare it to these comparable sales: [list sales]. Is the deal pricing at a premium or discount to market? What adjustments would bring it in line?" --- ## Phase 3: Professional Listing Package Creation You've found the deal, analyzed it, and now you need to present it — to partners, investors, or buyers. This is the most visible part of the workflow, and also the most time-consuming. ### Step 3.1: Property description writing A commercial property listing needs to communicate: - Property type and classification (Class A/B/C) - Key financial metrics (Cap Rate, NOI, rent rolls) - Physical attributes (square footage, ceiling height, loading, parking) - Tenant profile and lease structure - Value-add opportunities **AI workflow:** Feed the property details and investment analysis into an AI system. Ask it to generate a professional listing description optimized for your target buyer persona (institutional investor, owner-user, or syndicate). ### Step 3.2: Investment memorandum For larger deals, you'll need an investment memo — a structured document covering: - Executive summary - Market analysis - Property highlights - Financial projections - Risk factors - Recommendations **AI workflow:** Feed all your Phase 1 and Phase 2 outputs. Ask the AI to assemble a professional investment memorandum in your branding. ### Step 3.3: Multi-platform distribution Different platforms require different formats: - **LoopNet/Crexi**: Standardized listing format, photos, key metrics front-loaded - **Private buyer lists**: Custom email + PDF package - **Social media**: Brief highlight posts for LinkedIn **AI workflow:** Generate platform-specific versions of your listing in one go — no manual reformatting. --- ## The Time Savings: Before vs. After AI | Task | Traditional | With AI Workflow | Time Saved | |---|---|---|---| | Property research | 2–4 hours | 15–30 minutes | ~85% | | Investment analysis (NOI, Cap Rate, projections) | 4–8 hours | 30–60 minutes | ~85% | | Listing package creation | 3–5 hours | 15–30 minutes | ~85% | | Multi-platform adaptation | 1–2 hours | 5–10 minutes | ~90% | | **Total per deal** | **10–19 hours** | **1–2 hours** | **~90%** | --- ## What About Enterprise Tools?
